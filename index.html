Our Services

Here are the services we offer: education, community empowerment, skills development, social welfare, youth and women support, and health.

Food And Nutrition Service

  • To provide nutritious food and support services to individuals struggling with substance use and destitution, promoting health, dignity, and recovery.
  • Provide daily meals and snacks through our soup kitchen and food pantry programs and offer nutritional education and cooking classes to promote healthy eating habits.
  • Soup Kitchen: Daily snacks

Teenagers against Drug Abuse

  • This is the safe enabling environment for youth that is on schools by engaging them in this program which is a peer educational program.
  • We mostly provide services to marginalized, previously disadvantage and children coming from poor families without close family member’s support.

Community Outreach and Awareness Campaign on HIV/AIDS. TB and STIs and Substance Abuse:

  • This program empowers 5000 adolescents and young people with information and advice regarding children’s issues, awareness and social mobilization for access to health services, including: - HIV counselling and testing through HTS services

Sport, Art and Culture Adolescent and Youth Program

  • Use this program to make health related awareness campaigns during school sports, art or cultural days at school, department of basic education competition happening around Buffalo City Metropolitan Municipality.
  • Support all beneficiaries fostered by the organization with financial, care and support to join sport and/or art federations.

Work Integrated Learning (WIL) Youth Program:

  • Provides learnership, internship and short skills program to unemployed youth who are out of school, graduated from basic
  • Education or completed their higher education qualification from national certificate to bachelor’s degree.

A toll-free number

  • A toll-free telephone services offering counselling and support services to 1500 callers, mainly children, families and community members dealing with issues of substance abuse and gender-based violence and including facilitation of rehabilitation centre and safe house protection services.

Counselling and Support services

  • in our community-based centre and private practice our social workers provide 1000 counselling and support services to more than 1500 employees, abused, traumatised and substance abuse users as well as mental health clients.

Community awareness and Prevention Programmes

  • Empowers 20 000 children, adults, thereby preventing child abuse, teenagers against drug abuse, aftercare services for recoveries on substance abuse and ensuring early intervention strategies.

Community development

  • working with the local drug action committee, Masimanyane women’s support centre, Department of Social Development, Department of Education led by Department of Social Development,

Child development leadership Programme

  • Each community-based centre implements “Leaders” a junior leadership programme for 300 young people in schools for one year.

Training and development

  • Employees, youth, volunteers, professionals and community members working on field of substance abuse, gender-based violence and child protection

Positive Parenting

  • Positive Discipline

Special Projects

  • Mental and Wellness Programme – working with Department of Public works to deliver psychosocial services for their employees in the eastern cape

Youth Development and Social work graduate

  • Entrepreneurship, training volunteers and graduates in psychosocial skills and entrepreneurship to promote employment.

About Us

Lisebanzi Foundation was founded in 2016 by a dedicated social worker who had personally experienced the devastating effects of substance abuse. Her own child had struggled with drug addiction, prompting her to take action and create a support system for other families and communities affected by this issue.
